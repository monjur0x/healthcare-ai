# Key Clinical Trial Evidence: Landmark Studies in Cardiometabolic Medicine

## Diabetes and Cardiovascular Outcomes

### EMPA-REG OUTCOME (2016)
- **Drug**: Empagliflozin (SGLT2 inhibitor)
- **Population**: T2DM with established CVD, n=7020
- **Primary Outcome**: 3-point MACE (CV death, nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke)
- **Result**: HR 0.86 (14% relative risk reduction), p=0.04
- **Key Finding**: 38% reduction in CV death; 35% reduction in HF hospitalization
- **Significance**: First SGLT2i trial to show CV benefit - paradigm shift in diabetes treatment

### LEADER (2016)
- **Drug**: Liraglutide (GLP-1 receptor agonist)
- **Population**: T2DM with high CV risk, n=9340
- **Primary Outcome**: 3-point MACE
- **Result**: HR 0.87 (13% reduction), p<0.001
- **Additional**: 22% reduction in CV death, 15% reduction in all-cause mortality

### SUSTAIN-6 (2017)
- **Drug**: Semaglutide (GLP-1 RA, once weekly)
- **Population**: T2DM with CVD or high risk, n=3297
- **Primary Outcome**: 3-point MACE
- **Result**: HR 0.74 (26% reduction)
- **Notable**: 39% reduction in nonfatal stroke

## Heart Failure

### PARADIGM-HF (2014)
- **Comparison**: Sacubitril/Valsartan vs Enalapril in HFrEF
- **Population**: NYHA II-IV, LVEF ≤ 40%, n=8442
- **Result**: HR 0.80 for CV death/HF hospitalization
- **Significance**: Led to ARNI replacing ACEi as standard of care in HFrEF
- **Early termination** due to overwhelming benefit at median follow-up 27 months

### DAPA-HF (2019)
- **Drug**: Dapagliflozin (SGLT2i) in HFrEF
- **Population**: HFrEF ≤ 40% with/without diabetes, n=4744
- **Primary Outcome**: CV death/worsening HF
- **Result**: HR 0.74 (26% reduction), NNT = 21
- **Key Finding**: Benefit independent of diabetes status

### DELIVER (2022)
- **Drug**: Dapagliflozin in HFpEF (LVEF > 40%)
- **Population**: n=6263
- **Result**: HR 0.82 (18% reduction in CV death/worsening HF)
- **Significance**: Confirmed benefit extends to HFpEF population

### TOPCAT (2014)
- **Drug**: Spironolactone in HFpEF
- **Population**: n=3445
- **Mixed Result**: No overall significant benefit, but strong regional heterogeneity
- **Subgroup**: Americas showed significant benefit (HR 0.82)

## Chronic Kidney Disease

### CREDENCE (2019)
- **Drug**: Canagliflozin in diabetic CKD
- **Population**: T2DM + eGFR 30-90 + albuminuria, n=4401
- **Primary Outcome**: Renal composite (ESKD, doubling Cr, renal/CV death)
- **Result**: HR 0.70 (30% reduction) - early termination for efficacy

### DAPA-CKD (2020)
- **Drug**: Dapagliflozin in CKD (diabetic and non-diabetic)
- **Population**: eGFR 25-75, uACR ≥ 200 mg/g, n=4304
- **Result**: HR 0.61 (39% reduction in primary outcome)
- **Landmark**: First trial showing benefit regardless of diabetes status

### EMPA-KIDNEY (2022)
- **Drug**: Empagliflozin in CKD
- **Population**: eGFR ≥ 20 (broader than previous trials), n=6609
- **Result**: HR 0.72 (28% reduction in kidney disease progression or CV death)
- **Extension**: Confirmed benefit down to eGFR 20

### FIDELIO-DKD (2020)
- **Drug**: Finerenone (non-steroidal MRA) in diabetic CKD
- **Population**: T2DM + CKD, n=5734
- **Result**: HR 0.82 (18% reduction in kidney composite)
- **Additional**: 14% reduction in CV composite

## Acute Coronary Syndrome

### FOURIER (2017)
- **Drug**: Evolocumab (PCSK9 inhibitor)
- **Population**: Stable ASCVD on statin, n=27564
- **Result**: LDL reduced by 59%; HR 0.85 for MACE
- **Significance**: Confirmed PCSK9 inhibitors reduce CV events

### ODYSSEY Outcomes (2018)
- **Drug**: Alirocumab (PCSK9 inhibitor) post-ACS
- **Population**: Recent ACS on max statin, n=18924
- **Result**: HR 0.85 (15% reduction in primary endpoint)
- **Additional**: All-cause mortality reduced by 15%

### COMPASS (2017)
- **Regimen**: Rivaroxaban 2.5mg BID + Aspirin 100mg daily
- **Population**: Stable CAD/PAD, n=27395
- **Result**: HR 0.76 (24% reduction in CV death/stroke/MI)
- **Trade-off**: Increased major bleeding (but not fatal/intracranial)

## Antiplatelet Therapy

### PLATO (2009)
- **Drug**: Ticagrelor vs Clopidogrel in ACS
- **Population**: ACS (STEMI/NSTEMI), n=18624
- **Result**: HR 0.84 for CV death/MI/stroke; mortality benefit (HR 0.78)
- **Significance**: Ticagrelor became preferred P2Y12 inhibitor

### PEGASUS-TIMI 54 (2015)
- **Drug**: Ticagrelor long-term post-MI
- **Population**: MI 1-3 years prior + risk factors, n=21162
- **Result**: Reduced ischemic events but increased bleeding
- **Application**: Consider extended dual antiplatelet in high-risk patients

## Lipid Management

### IMPROVE-IT (2015)
- **Drug**: Ezetimibe added to statin post-ACS
- **Population**: n=18144
- **Result**: LDL < 54 achieved; HR 0.94 for composite endpoint
- **Proof of Concept**: Lower is better, even below 70 mg/dL

### ODYSSEY ESCAPE (2022)
- **Drug**: Alirocumab for recurrent hypercholesterolemia after PCSK9 antibody intolerance
- Demonstrates alternative approaches when standard therapies fail
