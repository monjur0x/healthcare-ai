# Hospital Protocol: Inpatient Diabetes Management

## Purpose
This protocol provides standardized guidance for managing blood glucose in hospitalized patients.

## Glycemic Targets
| Population | Target Range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Critically ill | 140-180 mg/dL |
| Non-critically ill | 140-180 mg/dL |
| Pregnancy (pre-existing DM) | 63-140 mg/dL |

## Insulin Protocols

### IV Insulin Infusion Protocol (Critically Ill)
Starting rate based on blood glucose: 1.0-4.0 units/hr
Adjustment: reduce by 50% if glucose drops > 100 mg/dL/hr or < 80 mg/dL
Stop if glucose < 70, give dextrose, restart at 50% prior rate

### Subcutaneous Basal-Bolus Protocol (Non-Critically Ill)
Basal insulin: glargine or degludec, 0.2 units/kg/day (normal weight)
Prandial insulin: lispro/aspart, 0.05 units/kg/meal
Reduce doses by 25% in elderly or CrCl < 60

### Correction Scale (Rapid-Acting Insulin)
141-180: 1 unit | 181-240: 2 units | 241-300: 4 units | >400: 8 units + call MD

## Hypoglycemia Management
Level 1 (54-69): 15g fast-acting carb orally, recheck in 15 min
Level 2 (<54): Dextrose 50% 25mL IV or Glucagon 1mg IM, recheck q15min until >90
Recurrent: reduce basal by 20%, investigate cause, consider endocrine consult

## Perioperative Management
SGLT2 inhibitors: Hold 3 days before surgery (ketoacidosis risk)
Metformin: Hold day of surgery
Major procedure: IV insulin preferred

## Common Pitfalls
Do not use sliding scale alone without basal insulin.
Do not hold basal insulin for NPO status.
Monitor for steroid-induced hyperglycemia.
