# KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease

## Definition and Classification

Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function, present for more than 3 months, with implications for health. CKD is classified based on:

### GFR Categories (KDIGO 2024)
- **G1**: Normal or high GFR (GFR ≥ 90 mL/min/1.73m²) - Kidney damage with normal GFR
- **G2**: Mildly decreased GFR (GFR 60-89) - Kidney damage with mild decrease
- **G3a**: Mildly-to-moderately decreased GFR (GFR 45-59)
- **G3b**: Moderately-to-severely decreased GFR (GFR 30-44)
- **G4**: Severely decreased GFR (GFR 15-29)
- **G5**: Kidney failure (GFR < 15)

### Albuminuria Categories
- **A1**: Normal to mildly increased (uACR < 30 mg/g)
- **A2**: Moderately increased (uACR 30-300 mg/g)
- **A3**: Severely increased (uACR > 300 mg/g)

## Risk Assessment

The KDIGO heat map combines GFR categories with albuminuria categories to estimate risk of progression:
- Low risk: G1-G2 with A1
- Moderately increased risk: G3a with A1, G1-G2 with A2
- High risk: G3b-G4 with A1-A2, G3a with A3
- Very high risk: G4-G5 with A3

## Screening Recommendations

### Who should be screened:
1. All patients with diabetes (annual uACR and serum creatinine)
2. All patients with hypertension
3. Patients with cardiovascular disease
4. Family history of CKD
5. Age > 60 years
6. Obesity, smoking, history of AKI

### Screening Tests:
1. **Serum creatinine** with eGFR calculation (CKD-EPI 2021 equation preferred)
2. **Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (uACR)** - first morning void preferred
3. **Cystatin C** when creatinine-based eGFR is less accurate

## Management Principles

### Blood Pressure Control
- Target SBP < 120 mm Hg (standardized office measurement)
- RAAS inhibition (ACEi or ARB) recommended in patients with:
  - Severely increased albuminuria (A3)
  - Moderately increased albuminuria (A2) with diabetes
  - Hypertension not controlled by other agents
- SGLT2 inhibitors recommended for G1-G4 with A2-A3 regardless of diabetes status

### Glycemic Control in Diabetic CKD
- HbA1c target: individualized, generally < 7.0%
- Metformin: continue if eGFR ≥ 30, dose-reduce if eGFR 30-44
- SGLT2i: dapagliflozin or empagliflozin for eGFR ≥ 20
- GLP-1 receptor agonists if glycemic targets not achieved despite metformin + SGLT2i

### Lipid Management
- Statins recommended for all patients ≥ 50 years with CKD
- Not recommended for dialysis-dependent patients to start statins de novo

### Anemia Management
- Screen for anemia when Hb < 130 g/L (men) or < 120 g/L (women)
- Iron supplementation when TSAT ≤ 30% and ferritin ≤ 500 ng/mL
- ESAs when Hb < 100 g/L despite iron repletion

### Mineral Bone Disorder
- Monitor calcium, phosphate, PTH in G3a-G5
- Dietary phosphate restriction
- Phosphate binders when dietary restriction insufficient

### Referral to Nephrology
- Refer when: eGFR < 30, uACR ≥ 300 mg/g, progressive decline, persistent hematuria
- Urgent referral: eGFR < 15, hyperkalemia refractory, uremic symptoms

## Drug Dosing in CKD
| Drug | Adjustment | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Metformin | eGFR 30-44: max 1000mg/day; eGFR < 30: contraindicated | Monitor B12 |
| ACEi/ARB | Reduce dose in renal impairment; monitor K+ and Cr | Max evidence for proteinuria |
| SGLT2i | Continue until eGFR < 20 | Do not initiate if eGFR < 25 |
| NSAIDs | Avoid in CKD G3-G5 | Nephrotoxic |
| Gadolinium | Avoid group I agents if eGFR < 30 | NSF risk |

## Monitoring Schedule
| GFR Category | Frequency of monitoring |
|-------------|------------------------|
| G1-G2, A1 | Annual |
| G3a, A1-A2 | Every 6 months |
| G3b, A2-A3 | Every 3-4 months |
| G4, A2-A3 | Every 2-3 months |
| G5 | Monthly or per nephrologist |

## Key Clinical Trials
- **DAPA-CKD** (2020): Dapagliflozin reduced primary composite outcome by 39%
- **EMPA-KIDNEY** (2022): Empagliflozin reduced kidney disease progression by 28%
- **FLOW** (2024): Semaglutide reduced kidney events in diabetic CKD
