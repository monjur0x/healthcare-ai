# WHO Guideline for the Pharmacological Treatment of Hypertension in Adults

## Overview
This guideline provides evidence-based recommendations for the initiation of pharmacological treatment of hypertension in adults. It is based on the 2021 update of the WHO guideline.

## Definition and Classification

Hypertension is defined as:
- Office systolic blood pressure ≥ 140 mm Hg AND/OR diastolic BP ≥ 90 mm Hg on two separate measurements

### Classification:
| Category | Systolic (mm Hg) | Diastolic (mm Hg) |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Normal | < 120 | < 80 |
| Elevated | 120-129 | < 80 |
| Stage 1 HTN | 130-139 | 80-89 |
| Stage 2 HTN | ≥ 140 | ≥ 90 |

## Treatment Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Initiation Threshold
WHO recommends initiating antihypertensive drug treatment in adults with:
- SBP ≥ 140 mm Hg and/or DBP ≥ 90 mm Hg
- Existing cardiovascular disease AND SBP 130-139 mm Hg

### Recommendation 2: Laboratory Testing Before Initiation
Before starting pharmacological treatment, assess:
- Serum creatinine with eGFR
- Serum potassium
- Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio
- Fasting glucose/HbA1c
- Lipid profile
- ECG

### Recommendation 3: Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
Assess CVD risk before treatment initiation:
- Use validated risk prediction tools where available
- Risk assessment helps guide intensity of treatment

### Recommendation 4: First-Line Drug Classes
The following classes should be used as initial treatment:
1. **Thiazide/thiazide-like diuretics**
2. **ACE inhibitors** or **ARBs**
3. **Long-acting calcium channel blockers**

### Recommendation 5: Combination Therapy
- Start combination therapy when baseline BP ≥ 20/10 mm Hg above target
- Combination therapy preferred over monotherapy titration for faster control
- Single-pill combination improves adherence

### Recommended Combinations:
1. ACEi + CCB, OR
2. ARB + CCB, OR
3. ACEi + Thiazide, OR
4. ARB + Thiazide

**Do NOT combine:**
- ACEi + ARB together
- ACEi or ARB + direct renin inhibitor

### Recommendation 6: Target Blood Pressure
- Target office BP < 140/90 mm Hg for all patients
- Target SBP < 130 mm Hg if tolerated (SPRINT trial evidence)

### Recommendation 7: Monitoring
- Monthly follow-up until target achieved
- Every 3-6 months once at goal
- Assess adherence, side effects, and comorbidities at each visit

## Special Populations

### Diabetes
- Target BP < 130/80 mm Hg
- ACEi or ARB preferred (renal protection)
- Monitor renal function closely

### CKD
- ACEi or ARB first-line
- More aggressive monitoring of potassium and creatinine
- Avoid NSAIDs

### Elderly (> 65 years)
- Same targets as younger patients if tolerated
- Start lower doses; titrate more slowly
- Be vigilant for orthostatic hypotension

### Pregnancy
- Labetalol, nifedipine ER, or methyldopa are safe choices
- ACEi and ARB are CONTRAINDICATED in pregnancy
- Target < 140/90 mm Hg

## Lifestyle Modifications (Non-pharmacological)
1. Reduce sodium intake to < 2 g/day sodium (< 5 g/day salt)
2. Increase potassium intake through diet (3.5-5 g/day) unless contraindicated
3. Weight loss if overweight (target BMI 20-25 kg/m²)
4. DASH diet (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension)
5. Physical activity: ≥ 150 minutes moderate-intensity per week
6. Limit alcohol: ≤ 2 drinks/day men, ≤ 1 drink/day women

## Key Evidence Base
| Trial | Population | Intervention | Result |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| SPRINT | High-risk, non-diabetic | Intensive SBP < 120 | 25% ↓ primary CV outcome |
| ADVANCE | Type 2 diabetes | Perindopril+Indapamide | 9% ↓ macro/microvascular |
| HYVET | Age ≥ 80 | Indapamide ± perindopril | 34% ↓ HF, 21% ↓ death |
| PROGRESS | Prior stroke/TIA | Perindopril-based | 28% ↓ recurrent stroke |
